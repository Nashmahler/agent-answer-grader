Agent Answer Grader

Can an AI judge reliably catch an AI support agent's mistakes? I wrote a policy for a fictional credit union, 30 agent replies (18 with planted problems), and labeled every one by hand. Then Claude graded them and had to quote the exact policy line behind each verdict. Code checks the quote; if it isn't real, the verdict becomes "needs review."

Agent Sorry about the lost card. I've noted it as lost, so it's locked now for your protection. A replacement is $10, and if you have Premier Checking that fee is waived once per calendar year. Judge The reply's facts match the policy: $10 fee, waived once per calendar year with Premier Checking, 7 to 10 business days standard, $25 for 2-day expedited, and the lost card is locked. It shares no account-specific details, needs no escalation, and the tone is courteous.

What I'd do next

  • Grade real transcripts, not just single replies: most agent failures happen across a whole conversation.
  • Add a second, cheaper model as a first pass and send only uncertain cases to the stronger judge.
  • Grow the test set to cover more of the policy, especially edge cases where two rules interact.
  • Track agreement over time, so a policy or model change that hurts the judge shows up immediately.
